## 5. What these checks do NOT establish

- Real fit: every bought-part envelope and mounting position is a placeholder (`# MEASURE`). The QW01 size is the user's approximate estimate; section 3d checks accommodation envelopes, not the real bank.
- Excluded contact pairs (section 2) are not proven to fit. They are listed with their overlap volume for review.
- Continuous motion clearance: caster and head checks are discrete samples (section 3b).
- Cable routing, bend radius and cable twist through the neck.
- Fastener engagement and strength: screw holes are modelled, thread engagement and pull-out are not.
- Printability (no slicer run), strength (no FEA), toy-standard compliance.
- The camera's real field of view, focus and image quality (section 3c is geometry only).
