Controls and limits

Drag to orbit. Shift-drag or right-drag to pan. Scroll to zoom. Touch: one finger orbits, two fingers pan and pinch. Click a part to inspect it. Focus the model and use arrow keys to orbit, + / − to zoom, Home to fit.

+X points forward, +Y is the robot's left, +Z is up. The floor is Z=0. Each cut retains the side shown by its selector. Hidden parts are also absent from the cut faces.

Exact assembled triangles from the v0.3 parametric model, including mirrored instances. Bought parts are PLACEHOLDER envelopes. The atmospheric module placement, switch holder, cables, hook-and-loop straps, screws, adhesives and keep-out volumes are not modelled here. No physical fit, strength or printability is established.
